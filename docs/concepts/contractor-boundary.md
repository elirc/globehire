# Contractor lifecycle boundary

Contractors and employees share people-facing workflows but not the same legal lifecycle. GlobeHire gives contractor engagements their own activation requirements and invoice state machine instead of adding contractor flags to employment.

Activation requires both an agreement and a classification review. Invoices progress through submitted, approved or disputed, and paid states. Disputes require evidence, while payment requires approval.

The classification rule here is a fictional workflow checkpoint, not a legal classification engine. Real assessments belong behind versioned policy and expert-review boundaries.
